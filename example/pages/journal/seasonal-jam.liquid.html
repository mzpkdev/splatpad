We don’t ask the fruit to fit the menu. We let the menu move with the fruit.

Our jam starts at the Saturday market. If the strawberries smell like June from three stalls away, they become clouds and thumbprint cookies. When apricots arrive, the morning buns get a bright, tart center.

We cook in small copper pans with just enough sugar to hold the season for a little longer. The batches stay loose, vivid, and different from one week to the next.

By autumn, plums and pears take over. Then citrus carries us through winter until the whole sweet cycle starts again.
