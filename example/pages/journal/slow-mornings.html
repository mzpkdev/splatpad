Flour, water, salt, time. Three ingredients do the visible work. The fourth does nearly everything else.

Every Alder sourdough begins the afternoon before it reaches the shelf. We mix gently, fold the dough through the evening, then let the cold settle everything down overnight.

That long rest builds flavor without making the loaf heavy. By dawn, the dough is lively, elastic, and ready for a hot oven. There’s no shortcut hiding behind the flour bins. Just a schedule designed around what the bread needs.

It’s slower than the efficient version. It also tastes better. We’re comfortable with that trade.
